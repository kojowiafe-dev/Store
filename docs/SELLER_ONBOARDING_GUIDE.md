# 🛍️ Campus Store — Student Seller's Guide

> **Welcome to Campus Store!**  
> This guide explains how to set up your digital storefront and start selling to fellow students across the University of Ghana, Legon. No technical background required — it takes less than 5 minutes to get started! 🎉

---

## 1. What is Campus Store?

Campus Store is a student-first product discovery network. 

Instead of posting the same photos every day to your WhatsApp status or pasting flyers in noisy hall group chats, Campus Store gives you **one personal digital storefront** where students can find everything you sell.

Buyers can search for items by name, browse categories, see what's currently available in your hall, and tap a button to message you directly on WhatsApp to buy.

---

## 3. How Much Does It Cost?

**Campus Store is 100% free for student sellers during the pilot.**
- Zero sign-up fees.
- Zero listing fees.
- Zero transaction fees or commissions.

You keep 100% of your earnings.

---

## 4. How Do Buyers Contact Me?

1. When a buyer finds your product, they tap the green **"Chat on WhatsApp"** button.
2. WhatsApp opens on their phone with a pre-filled message addressed to you:
   > *"Hello, I found your [Product Title] on Campus Store. Is it still available?"*
3. You chat normally on WhatsApp, agree on where to meet or deliver, and collect payment directly (cash, Mobile Money / MoMo).

> **Privacy Note:** When a buyer taps the WhatsApp button, they will see your WhatsApp phone number to message you. Campus Store does not show your number as plain text on the page, but it is part of the WhatsApp chat link.

## 5. How Do I Sign Up? (Under 5 Minutes)

1. **Go to `/seller/register`** on the Campus Store website.
2. **Enter your details:**
   - Your full name
   - Your email address (this will be your login)
   - A password (minimum 8 characters)
   - Your WhatsApp number (e.g. `+233501234567` — double-check this!)
3. **Verify your email:** Click the confirmation link sent to your inbox.
4. **Set up your storefront:**
   - **Store Name:** (e.g. *"Ama's Delights"*)
   - **Storefront Link:** Choose a unique web link (e.g. `campusstore.com/store/amas-delights`). You can share this link on your WhatsApp status and bio!
   - **Your Hall:** Select your residence hall (e.g. *Commonwealth Hall*).
   - **Store Bio & Logo:** (Optional) Add a brief description of what you sell.
5. **Admin Approval:** Because we're in a pilot phase, our student team does a quick review to approve your account. Once approved, you can start publishing immediately!

---

## 6. How Do I Add a Product?

1. Open your **Seller Dashboard** and tap **"Add Product"**.
2. **Select Category:** Choose where your item belongs (Food, Clothing, etc.).
3. **Product Title:** Keep it clear and descriptive (e.g. *"Fresh Jollof Rice with Grilled Chicken"* or *"iPhone 20W Fast Charger"*).
4. **Price in GHS:** Enter the price in Ghana Cedis. If the price is negotiable, check the **"Price is Negotiable"** box.
5. **Description (Optional):** Mention portion sizes, colors, condition, or pickup location.
6. **Add Photos (Optional):** You can upload up to **3 photos**. Photos are automatically compressed so you don't burn mobile data.
7. Tap **"Publish Product"** — your item is immediately live!

---

## 7. Keeping Your Listings Current (Two-Phase Expiry)

To keep buyers trusting Campus Store, listings have built-in expiry periods so students never see sold-out or old food from last week:

- **Food Listings (24 Hours):**
  - For the first 20 hours, your food shows as **Available**.
  - At the 20-hour mark, it shows a yellow **"Confirm Availability"** badge so buyers know to check with you.
  - To keep it active, just open your dashboard and tap **"Yes, Still Available"**! It resets for another 24 hours.
  - If you don't confirm by 24 hours, it moves to **Expired** and is hidden automatically. You can reactivate it anytime with one tap.
- **Physical Goods (7 to 30 Days):**
  - Clothing and electronics stay live longer (7 to 30 days) before asking for confirmation.

---

## 8. What If I Run Out of Stock?

You have full control from your dashboard:
- **Mark as Sold Out:** If you sell your last portion or item, tap **"Mark Sold Out"**. It will immediately be hidden from the main search. When you restock, tap **"Back in Stock"**!
- **Pause a Listing:** Going to class or taking a break? Tap **"Pause"** to temporarily hide the product. Tap **"Resume"** whenever you're ready again.
- **Delete:** If you're no longer offering this product, tap **"Delete"** to remove it.

---

## 9. Tips for Successful Selling

1. **Share your store link:** Copy your storefront URL (`campusstore.com/store/your-name`) and put it in your WhatsApp bio or status.
2. **Clear titles:** Write titles that students search for (e.g. *"Dell Laptop Charger Type-C"* rather than just *"Charger"*).
3. **Good lighting:** Take product photos near a window or in daylight. Good photos get 3x more WhatsApp taps!
4. **Quick responses:** Respond promptly when a student messages your WhatsApp to close the sale.
